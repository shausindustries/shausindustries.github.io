Inside the hilt

Control: an Arduino Nano reads the switch on D2 and steps through off, igniting, on and idle, playing the matching sound on a DFPlayer Mini and lighting an RGB LED.

The floating-pin fix: a 10 kΩ pull-down from D2 to GND, plus a 500 ms start-up grace period before any state change is acted on.

Power: a LiPo pouch cell, charged over USB-C by a TP4056 board and boosted to 5 V by an MCP1640.

Board: the perfboard prototype redrawn in KiCad — power interface, sockets for the Nano and DFPlayer, and a gyro header.

Body: white PVC pipe with window cut-outs, a black ribbed grip, black bands, a metal push button with a lit ring, and 3D-printed end pieces.
