The flow

Simulate the SoC and its peripherals in Icarus Verilog and check waveforms in GTKWave.

Synthesise with Yosys, then run the automated OpenLane / OpenROAD flow on SkyWater 130nm (sky130_fd_sc_hd) to a placed, routed, tape-out-ready GDSII.
